================================================================================
                         INTERACTIVE FEATURES IN ACTION
================================================================================

1. CAROUSEL - Auto-Rotates Every 4 Seconds

   Slide 1: Step & Repeat Banners
   Slide 2: Retractable Stands
   Slide 3: Breakaway Banners
   Slide 4: Vinyl Banners

   • Click dots at bottom to jump to specific slide
   • Hover for smooth fade transition
   • Click "Shop Now" to jump to product section


2. CATEGORY FILTERS

   Click "Banners":        Shows 5 banner products
   Click "Stands":         Shows 4 stand products
   Click "Signs":          Shows 3 sign products
   Click "Decals":         Shows 3 decal products
   Click "Flags":          Shows 3 flag products
   Click "Table Covers":   Shows 3 table products
   Click "All Products":   Shows all 20 products


3. SORT OPTIONS

   Select from dropdown:
   • Sort by Popular (default)
   • Sort by Price (Low to High)
   • Sort by Price (High to Low)
   • Sort by Newest


4. PRODUCT CARDS

   Each card shows:
   ✓ Product image (high quality)
   ✓ Category label
   ✓ Product title
   ✓ Star rating (1-5 stars)
   ✓ Review count
   ✓ Price in orange
   ✓ "Customize Now" button

   Hover effect: Card lifts up with shadow effect


5. CLICK PRODUCT → MODAL POPUP

   Modal shows:
   • Full product image
   • Product title
   • Category
   • Price
   • "Customize Now" button

   Close: Click X button or click outside modal


6. PAGINATION

   Shows 12 products per page
   Navigate: Click page numbers [1] [2] [3]
   Current page highlights in orange


7. RESPONSIVE DESIGN

   Desktop (wide):    3 columns of products
   Tablet (medium):   2 columns of products
   Mobile (small):    1 column of products

   All content remains readable and beautiful
   on any device size

================================================================================
                              CUSTOMIZATION GUIDE
================================================================================

Edit website.html to customize:

1. COLORS
   Find: :root { --primary: #1a237e; --accent: #ff6f00; }
   Edit: Change hex color codes

2. CAROUSEL SLIDES
   Find: <div class="carousel-slide"...
   Edit: Change background-image URLs

3. PRODUCTS
   Find: const products = [
   Edit: Add/remove product objects
   Each product needs: id, name, category, image, price, rating, reviews

4. CATEGORIES IN FILTERS
   Find: <button class="filter-btn"...
   Edit: Change onclick="filterCategory('...')

5. COMPANY INFO
   Find: Footer section
   Edit: Phone, email, address

6. NAVIGATION LINKS
   Find: <nav><a href=...
   Edit: Change URLs or add new links
